Reporting a puzzle problem

Please include the Daily puzzle date or say that you were playing Random, the row and column clues, the Pokémon and form you selected, and what happened. Your browser and device type can help with a display problem. A screenshot is optional; remove personal information before sending one.

Artwork and content inquiries

Identify the material and its location on the site, explain your concern, and provide a way to respond. SuPoku is an independent fan project; it cannot provide support for official Pokémon games or accounts.
