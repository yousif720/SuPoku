Terms & credits

SuPoku is a free fan-made trivia puzzle. These notes explain its intended use and the sources behind it.

Playing fairly

Please use the game for personal puzzle play. Do not disrupt the service, automate requests to manipulate answer statistics, or attempt to access systems or data beyond the public game features.

Game data and availability

Answers are evaluated using the game’s dataset. Pokémon mechanics vary between games, and data errors or omissions are possible. Rarity is an estimate from a fan survey and submitted answers, not an official ranking. Features and data may change, and archive grids are regenerated from the current dataset, so an old date can differ slightly after a data update. Please report suspected errors through the contact page.

Data and artwork credits

  • Pokédex data: PokeAPI.
  • Starting rarity estimates: the 2025 Favourite Pokémon survey results compiled by Sparklez Squad.
  • Pokémon sprites and artwork: PokeAPI sprites repository.
  • Locally mirrored type icons: the typedle project, as recorded in this project’s source documentation.
  • Scapholène typeface by Scapholène, used unchanged with its original license.
